## Roles, derivation and formal proof scope

The research team (R002/Codex) developed the classification and its
negative surface proof. The coordinator (Claude Code) supplied the
positive construction and independent challenge specification. The
verification team R004 separately derived a general prose proof without
reading the research team's proof, construction or code, from the known
claim and its own prior work. This is independent derivation, not a blind
test or a third independent Lean development. One AI coordinator reviewed
the alternative prose arguments. Same-agent alternative implementations
and shared-constructor traces are not additional reviewers.

The exact main formal entry is `ClaudeMain.main_full`, against
`ClaudeChallenge.main`, with graph `ClaudeChallenge.gcGraph` and predicate
`ClaudeChallenge.criterion`. The full source closure and proved graph
isomorphisms include ordinary parent-edge points. The historical primitive
checkpoint is `ClaudeMain.main_primitive`. The specification is still a
model whose intended published meaning is a distinct correspondence issue.

The coordinator's full-classification Comparator accepted the same
supplied proof in six kernels (standard Lean, paranoid Lean, lean4lean,
nanoda, con-leche, con-ron), with `propext`, `Classical.choice` and
`Quot.sound` as the allowed axioms. This is not six independently derived
proofs. That run covers the nonprimitive case but not the later minimum
and ray declarations. macOS runs had no Linux sandbox/process isolation.
The primitive run has its own older scope. Sources, receipts, challenge
and proof navigation, pinned toolchain and original public derivatives
remain in FORMALIZATION.md and the structured metadata.

`ClaudeCor.minimum_at` and its data/absence/below-bound lemmas establish
the primitive minimum using kernel `decide`. `ClaudeCor.criterion_ray`
and `R002.A3Release.path_ray` supply existence of a threshold/positive
period, including nonprimitive points. The executable algorithm is not
certified. Density is a separate prose proof draft and remains outside
the formalization target. All 57 claim records, status values and human
review flags, and all 165 Lean sources, are unchanged.

On 19 October the owner reported confirming the independent statement's
meaning with a reading guide. This was neither proof peer review nor
novelty review. The confirmation procedure ended on 20 October; the old
record is retained, not introduced as a new approval step.
